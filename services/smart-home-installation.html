FAQ

Smart home questions

My switches don't have a neutral wire — can I still get smart switches?
Yes. Lutron Caseta works without a neutral. For other brands (Leviton, Kasa, Eve), we can pull a neutral from the nearest junction during install. Most older Austin homes were built without neutrals at switch boxes — we deal with this on most installs.
What's the difference between Lutron Caseta and Lutron RA3?
Caseta is DIY-friendly, runs on a wireless hub, and is great for retrofitting 8–25 switches. RadioRA 3 is the whole-home system — keypads, scenes, advanced programming, professional dealer-only design. RA3 starts at ~$5–8k installed and goes up from there. Most Austin homeowners are happiest with Caseta for the price/performance ratio.
Do you install Span panels?
Yes, frequently. Span replaces your existing panel with a fully smart panel — every circuit is monitored, every breaker is remotely controllable, and EV/solar/battery integration is built in. Cost is $5,500–$8,500 installed depending on circuit count. Best for solar, EV, and battery backup households.
Will the smart home keep working if internet goes out?
For local-control systems (Lutron Caseta/RA3 with hub, Apple Home with HomePod hub, Home Assistant) — yes, scenes and automations keep working. For cloud-only systems (older Wi-Fi switches without hubs) — they often stop working when internet goes down. We design around local-first control for reliability.
Can you work with my smart home consultant or AV integrator?
Absolutely — we work alongside several Austin-area integrators. We handle line-voltage and code-required work; they handle programming, AV, and low-voltage system design. Clean division of responsibility.
