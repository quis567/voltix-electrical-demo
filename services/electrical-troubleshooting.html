Common Issues

If you're seeing this, call us today.

Tripped breakers

Same circuit keeps tripping? Could be overload, short, or failing breaker. We trace and fix.

Flickering lights

Voltage drops, loose connections, or service issue. We diagnose with meters and thermal imaging.

Dead outlets

Tripped GFCI, broken receptacle, or bad wiring upstream. Diagnosed and replaced same-day.

Burning smell

Stop using the circuit. Call now. This is a fire-risk emergency we treat as priority dispatch.

Sparking outlets

Big or repeated sparks indicate a wiring failure. Turn off the circuit and call us immediately.

Water damage

Leak hit electrical? We isolate, dry, test, and replace what's needed before re-energizing.

Hot switches/outlets

Heat means resistance — and resistance means imminent failure. Replace before it fails.

Partial outage

Half the house dark? Often a failed neutral or service issue. We diagnose and coordinate with Austin Energy.

FAQ

Troubleshooting questions

Can you fix it in one visit?
About 80% of the time, yes. Trucks carry common breakers, GFCI/AFCI outlets, switches, wire, and connectors. Specialty parts (older Square D HOM panel breakers, specific Tesla connectors, etc.) may need a return visit — but we never leave a hazardous situation, and a return visit is scheduled within 24 hours.
Is the diagnostic fee credited toward repair?
For Same-Day Diagnostic ($249) — yes, fully credited. For Standard Diagnostic ($149) — credited toward repairs over $400. Emergency calls — fee is not credited because of after-hours dispatch costs.
What if you can't fix the problem?
If we can't reproduce or root-cause an intermittent issue, we'll install monitoring (current/voltage logging) for 7–14 days at no extra charge. We won't bill for repairs we can't verify fixed the actual problem.
Should I turn off the circuit before you arrive?
If you smell burning, see sparking, or feel hot devices — yes, turn off the circuit at the breaker. For flickering lights or random trips, leave it on so we can observe live.
Do you handle insurance claims?
Yes. For damage from electrical fires, surge events, or storms, we provide insurer-ready reports including photos, code references, and itemized repair scope. We've worked with State Farm, USAA, Allstate, Liberty Mutual, and most major Texas carriers.
