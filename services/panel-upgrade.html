Do You Need One?

Signs your panel is due for an upgrade

Most Austin homes built before 2000 were wired for a much smaller electrical load than what today's lifestyle demands. EV chargers, induction ranges, heat pumps, hot tubs, and home offices all draw heavily — and an undersized panel becomes a daily headache.

  • Breakers trip frequently or feel warm to the touch
  • You're installing an EV charger, generator, or hot tub
  • Lights flicker when major appliances start up
  • Your panel is a Federal Pacific, Zinsco, or 60+ years old
  • Home inspection flagged code violations or undersized service
  • You're remodeling and need to add 2+ new circuits

Code Compliance

NEC. Austin Energy. AHJ inspector. Every box checked.

A panel upgrade is not just a hardware swap. The NEC has changed every three years for the last two decades — and Austin's local amendments add another layer. Voltix work is built to pass inspection on the first try.

  • Permits pulled with City of Austin Development Services
  • Coordinated meter pull/reset with Austin Energy
  • AFCI/GFCI protection per current NEC requirements
  • Whole-home surge protection installed at the panel
  • Proper bonding and grounding to local code

FAQ

Panel upgrade questions, answered

How long does a panel upgrade take?
Most residential 100A → 200A upgrades are completed in 6–10 hours. Power is off for 2–4 hours during the actual cutover. We schedule meter pulls in advance with Austin Energy so you're not without power overnight.
Do you handle the permit and inspection?
Yes — completely. We pull the City of Austin permit, coordinate with Austin Energy for meter pull/reset, and schedule the AHJ inspector. You don't make a single phone call.
What happens if the inspector fails the work?
In 18 years and 2,400+ projects, this has never happened on a panel upgrade. If it ever did, we fix it at no cost and re-schedule the inspection. Our quote is the price you pay.
Do I need a 200A or 400A service?
For most Austin homes 2,500–4,000 sq ft, 200A is plenty — even with an EV charger and heat pump. Larger homes, ADUs, or homes with EV + generator + pool + heat pump may benefit from 400A. We do a load calculation during the assessment so you don't pay for capacity you don't need.
Can you do a partial upgrade?
In some cases — adding a sub-panel or new circuit can defer a full upgrade. We'll tell you honestly if a partial fix solves your real problem.
Do you offer financing?
Yes. We offer 0% financing for 12 or 24 months through GreenSky and Sunbelt for qualified buyers. See financing options →
What if my home has aluminum or knob-and-tube wiring?
A panel upgrade alone won't make legacy wiring safe. We'll assess and recommend whether you need a panel upgrade only, panel + selective rewiring, or a phased full rewire.
Will I need any drywall or paint repair?
Sometimes — particularly if the meter base is moving or new conduit needs to enter the home. We patch any drywall and color-match paint before we leave. No third-party scheduling needed.
