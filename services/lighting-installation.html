LED Tip

Your dimmer is probably the problem.

If your LED bulbs are flickering, buzzing, or dropping out at low brightness, the cause is usually a dimmer mismatch — not the bulbs. Old incandescent dimmers are wrong for LED loads, and not all "LED-compatible" dimmers handle every LED driver.

During lighting installs we match dimmer model to fixture type, verify the load curve, and run a 5-minute soak test before we leave. Lutron Caseta and Lutron Diva are our defaults for retrofits — they handle nearly every LED driver on the market.

Color Temperature Quick Guide

  • 2700K (Warm White): Living rooms, bedrooms, dining. Cozy, fireplace-like.
  • 3000K (Soft White): Kitchens, bathrooms, hallways. Slightly cleaner.
  • 3500K (Neutral): Home offices, studios. Balanced for tasks.
  • 4000K+ (Cool White): Garages, workshops. Avoid in living areas — feels clinical.
  • Tunable White: Best of all worlds — adjust 2700K–5000K via app or scene. Worth the small premium.

FAQ

Lighting questions

How much for a typical kitchen recessed LED retrofit?
A typical 8–10 light retrofit in an existing finished ceiling runs $700–$1,400 — including fixtures (we use ELCO or Halo wafer LEDs), wiring, and dimmer. New installations through unfinished ceiling joists are typically less. We provide a flat-rate written quote during the estimate.
Will you cause drywall damage?
For wafer LED retrofits in existing ceilings — minimal. Wafers fit through a 4" or 6" hole, and we cut clean. For new ceiling fans where there's no existing electrical box, we cut access to install joist bracing — that gets patched and painted before we leave (we do the patching ourselves).
Do I provide the fixtures?
Either way works. We can supply ELCO, Halo, Hubbell, Lutron, Lutron Caseta, Hunter, Big Ass Fans, Casablanca, Hinkley landscape lights, and most other major brands at competitive prices. Or you can buy them and we install. We'll tell you if a fixture you've picked has known dimming or compatibility issues.
Can you install a chandelier in a tall foyer?
Yes. We have lift equipment for ceilings up to 24'. Foyer chandelier installs typically run $350–$800 for the labor plus the fixture and any electrical box / fan brace as needed.
Do you do landscape lighting design?
For Voltix-installed projects, yes — we design lighting layout based on your home's architecture, walking paths, and security needs. For larger custom landscape lighting work, we partner with a local lighting designer for the artistic side and handle the electrical install.
