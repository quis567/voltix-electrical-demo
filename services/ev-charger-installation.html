Why Charge at Home?

Wake up to a full battery, every day.

Roughly 80% of EV charging happens at home. A Level 2 charger replenishes ~25–40 miles of range per hour — versus ~3–5 miles per hour from a standard outlet. For most Austin commuters, that means leaving the house every morning with a full battery and never thinking about public charging.

  • 10x faster than Level 1 (standard outlet)
  • Charges overnight on off-peak rates
  • Adds resale value to your home
  • Future-proofs for second EV or plug-in hybrid

Common Question

"Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?"

Maybe. A typical Level 2 charger draws 48A on a 60A circuit — and most homes built before 2010 only have 100A or 125A service. Adding a charger to an already-near-capacity panel can trigger nuisance trips or require load-management hardware.

During the assessment we run a load calculation. About 40% of our EV customers need a panel upgrade. The other 60% are fine on existing service, sometimes with a smart load-management device that throttles the charger when other big loads run.

Texas EV Incentives

  • Austin Energy Plug-In EVerywhere: Rebates available for residential Level 2 chargers in the Austin Energy service area.
  • Federal Tax Credit (30C): 30% of equipment + install costs, up to $1,000, in eligible non-urban or low-income census tracts.
  • Texas Gas Utility & Co-op Rebates: Some local utilities offer additional incentives — we'll check what applies to your address.

We'll help you check eligibility and complete paperwork at no charge.

FAQ

EV charger questions, answered

How long does installation take?
Most residential Level 2 charger installs take 4–8 hours. If we need to add a panel upgrade or run conduit through finished walls, expect a second visit and a longer scope.
Do you handle the permitting?
Yes — completely. We pull the City of Austin electrical permit, schedule any required inspections with the AHJ, and coordinate with Austin Energy if a meter pull is needed. You don't make a single phone call.
What's the difference between Tesla Wall Connector and ChargePoint?
For Tesla owners, the Wall Connector is the cleanest pairing — native NACS connector, app integration, slightly faster. For non-Tesla EVs (Ford, Hyundai, Kia, Rivian, etc.), ChargePoint or Wallbox are usually the better choice with a J1772 connector. Tesla recently opened the Wall Connector to non-Tesla EVs via adapter — but most non-Tesla owners prefer the universal J1772.
Can you integrate with my smart home / Home Assistant?
Yes. ChargePoint, Wallbox, and Tesla all support API/Matter integrations. We can also wire in dedicated current-monitoring (Emporia, Span) so your charger automatically throttles when the AC kicks on or you're cooking on the induction range.
What's the warranty?
Hardware: 3 years (Tesla, ChargePoint, Wallbox manufacturer warranty). Workmanship: 2 years from Voltix. We register the warranty for you on install day.
Do you offer financing?
Yes — 0% for 12 months on installs over $2,000 through GreenSky. See financing options →
Can I install the charger myself?
Texas requires Level 2 charger circuits to be installed by a licensed electrician. A DIY install also voids the manufacturer warranty and most home insurance policies. The cost difference between DIY and professional install is small once you account for permits, inspection, and the panel work most homes need.
