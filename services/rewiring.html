Why Rewire?

Old wiring isn't just inconvenient — it's dangerous.

Electrical fires kill ~500 Americans every year. Most are caused by aged or improperly installed wiring — knob-and-tube (1900–1940s), 1960s-era aluminum branch circuits, and cloth-insulated wire that's lost its insulation. Insurance carriers are increasingly refusing to write or renew policies on homes with these issues.

  • Knob-and-tube wiring (homes pre-1950)
  • Aluminum branch circuits (~1965–1972)
  • Cloth-insulated wire (degraded insulation)
  • Insurance non-renewal due to wiring issues
  • Failed home inspection during sale or refinance
  • Major remodel triggering full code update

FAQ

Rewiring questions, answered

How long does a whole-home rewire take?
For a typical 2,000 sq ft Austin home, plan on 8–14 working days. We work room-by-room, restore power each evening, and the family stays in the house. Larger or more complex homes (multiple stories, plaster walls, additions) can take 3–4 weeks.
Do I need to move out during a rewire?
Almost never. We design a phased schedule so each evening the home has full power and is fully functional. The only exception is if we discover hidden hazards (active K&T behind insulation, fire damage) that require more aggressive containment.
How much drywall damage is unavoidable?
For a phased "fish" rewire, we typically need to cut access at switch boxes, ceiling fixtures, and a few "fish points" per room (hand-sized openings). We patch and paint every cut ourselves before we leave — no third-party drywaller scheduling. For homes with plaster walls or insulation-packed cavities, more cuts are sometimes required, and we'll show you a marked plan before any cutting starts.
Will my home be code-compliant after the rewire?
Yes — beyond compliant. New code requires AFCI on bedrooms and most living spaces, GFCI on kitchens, baths, garages, exteriors, and laundry. We bring every rewired circuit up to current NEC and Austin local code, and the work passes AHJ inspection.
Should I do a panel upgrade at the same time?
Usually yes. If the existing panel is 60A, 100A, Federal Pacific, or Zinsco, replacing it during the rewire is far cheaper than doing it separately. We bundle the costs together and provide a combined quote. View panel upgrade →
Will my insurance company accept the work?
Yes. We provide a certified rewire report acceptable to all major Texas carriers. We'll also work directly with your insurance agent or underwriter to confirm specific documentation requirements before we start.

Yes. 0% for 12 months on rewires up to $15,000, or extended terms up to 84 months at competitive rates for larger projects. See financing options →
