Is It an Emergency?

Common emergencies — and what to do right now

If you see any of these, treat it as urgent. Some are immediately dangerous; all are call-now situations.

Burning smell or smoke

Wiring is failing or arcing. Risk of fire is immediate.

Do: Turn off the main breaker. Get everyone out. Call us. Call 911 if smoke is visible.

Sparking outlets or switches

Single small spark when plugging in is normal. Repeated sparks or large arcs are not.

Do: Stop using that outlet. Turn off the circuit at the breaker. Call us.

Hot outlets or switches

Heat means resistance, which means impending failure.

Do: Turn off the circuit. Don't ignore it. Call us today.

Power outage (your home only)

Neighbors have power but you don't? Could be a service issue or main breaker failure.

Do: Check the main breaker, then call us. We coordinate with Austin Energy if needed.

Half the house dark

Often a failed neutral on the service drop. Causes voltage swings that can damage electronics.

Do: Unplug sensitive electronics. Call us — this is urgent.

Water + electrical

Roof leak hit a fixture. Plumbing leak above the panel. Flooded basement with outlets.

Do: Don't touch wet electrical. Turn off the main breaker if safe to do so. Call us.

Repeated breaker trips

A breaker that resets but immediately trips again means a short or ground fault.

Do: Leave it off. Don't keep resetting (causes arc damage). Call us.

Storm / vehicle damage

Tree on the service drop. Car hit your meter. Power line down on your property.

Do: Stay 30+ feet from downed lines. Call 911 first if line is on the ground, then us.
