Pflugerville Specifics

What we see most in Pflugerville

Pflugerville is one of the youngest housing markets in central Texas — over 70% of homes were built after 2000. Most neighborhoods we work in (Falcon Pointe, Avalon, Blackhawk, Heritage Park, Highland Park, Springbrook) feature 100A or 125A panels that worked fine for 2005 but are stretched today by EVs, hot tubs, electric pool heaters, and 90+ degree summer AC loads.

The most common Pflugerville call we get isn't "my breaker tripped" — it's "I want a Tesla and I just realized my panel is full." We do the load calc, recommend either a panel upgrade or a smart load-management device (Emporia, Tesla Energy Gateway), and get you charging.

Pflugerville also has a sizeable post-2021 generator demand. The combination of 130 and I-35 corridor weather exposure, distance from Austin Energy resources, and a high concentration of work-from-home professionals has made standby generators one of our top services here.

Permits go through the City of Pflugerville Building Department. Travis County areas just outside city limits permit through Travis County. We pull both. Pflugerville inspectors typically schedule within 5–7 days of permit issue.

FAQ — Pflugerville

Pflugerville-specific questions

What's your typical response time in Pflugerville?
For non-emergency, same-day or next-day. For emergency dispatch from our central Austin office to Pflugerville: ~40 minutes via I-35 or 130, depending on traffic.
Do you handle the Pflugerville permit yourself?
Yes — completely. City of Pflugerville Building Department for in-city addresses, Travis County for unincorporated areas. We pull every permit and schedule every inspection.
Will you install a generator in my Falcon Pointe HOA?
Yes. We've installed in Falcon Pointe, Avalon, Blackhawk, and Heritage Park — all HOA-approved. We provide the manufacturer specs, placement diagram, and noise data needed for HOA review and handle the application paperwork.
Do I need a panel upgrade if I'm adding a Tesla?
Maybe. About half of Pflugerville EV installs we do require a panel upgrade. The other half are fine with the existing service plus a smart load-management device that throttles the charger when other major loads run. We do a real load calc — no guessing — during the assessment.
Do you serve Hutto and Manor too?
Yes. Both are part of our regular service area. Hutto especially has been growing fast, and we install regularly there. Manor coverage is full — both city limits and Travis County unincorporated.
