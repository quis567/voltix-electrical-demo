FAQ — Austin

Austin-specific questions

Are you licensed in Austin / Texas?
Yes. James Reyes holds Texas Master Electrician license TECL #29874. Voltix is a Texas-licensed Electrical Contractor and registered with the City of Austin for permit pulling.
What permits does Austin require for a panel upgrade?
An electrical permit through City of Austin Development Services. We pull it. Austin Energy also has to coordinate the meter pull/reset, which we schedule. Final inspection is performed by an AHJ inspector — also coordinated by us.
What's your typical response time in Austin?
For non-emergency, same-day or next-day. For emergencies, our average dispatch from the central Austin office is under 30 minutes within city limits, ~45 minutes to suburbs.
Are EV charger rebates available in Austin?
Yes — Austin Energy's Plug-In EVerywhere program offers rebates for residential Level 2 charger installs in the Austin Energy service area. We help you complete the application as part of every install.
Do you handle historic preservation rules in protected neighborhoods?
Yes. For homes in NCCDs (Neighborhood Conservation Combining Districts) like Hyde Park or homes on the Austin Historic Landmark list, we coordinate with City of Austin Historic Preservation if exterior wiring or service entrance work would change the building's appearance.
