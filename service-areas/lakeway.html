Lakeway Specifics

Why Lakeway needs different electrical thinking

Lakeway is unlike the rest of the Austin metro. The homes are larger (median ~3,200 sq ft, plenty over 5,000), the lots are wooded and rocky, and the electrical service is mostly Pedernales Electric Cooperative (PEC) — not Austin Energy. Together, that means: bigger panels, longer service drops, generator priority, and a different permit path.

Lakeway has historically been one of the slowest power-restoration zones in Greater Austin during ice storms and high-wind events. That's made standby generators almost a default for new construction here. We've installed 90+ Generac and Kohler units in Lakeway, Flintrock Falls, Rough Hollow, and The Hills since 2021. Sizing tends to run larger (22kW–38kW) because of dual HVAC zones, well pumps, pool equipment, and the larger square footage.

EV charger installs in Lakeway often need 80A+ circuits because of the long runs from panel to garage or carport. Several Lakeway homes also feature dual-EV households, so we frequently install power-sharing chargers (Wallbox, Tesla Wall Connector with load management).

Permits go through the City of Lakeway Building Department for in-city addresses, and Travis County for unincorporated Hill Country. PEC service-side coordination is standard for new circuits, panel upgrades, and generator inter-tie. We handle all of it.

FAQ — Lakeway

Lakeway-specific questions

What's your typical response time in Lakeway?
For non-emergency, same-day or next-day. For emergency dispatch from our central Austin office to Lakeway: ~50 minutes via 360 or 71. We have a Master Electrician who lives in the area and can respond faster on weekend emergencies.
Can you handle PEC service work?
Yes. We coordinate directly with Pedernales Electric Cooperative for service drops, meter pulls/resets, and generator inter-tie inspections. We've worked with PEC on hundreds of Lakeway-area projects since 2010.
Do you install generators in Lakeway HOA neighborhoods?
Yes — frequently. Lakeway proper, The Hills, Flintrock Falls, Rough Hollow, and Spanish Oaks all have HOAs that allow standby generators with reasonable placement and screening. We submit the HOA paperwork on your behalf and provide manufacturer specs and noise data.
Do you serve Bee Cave and Spicewood?
Yes. Bee Cave is part of our regular service area. Spicewood we cover for projects but not always for service calls — call to confirm based on your specific address.
What about lake-area docks and boathouse electrical?
Yes. Dock electrical (boat lift, dock lights, outlet) requires special wet-location wiring and GFCI protection. We work with the LCRA permitting process for any work below the 715' contour. This is a niche we've done for decades.
