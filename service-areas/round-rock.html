Round Rock Specifics

What's different about Round Rock work

Round Rock is a city of newer-construction homes — most subdivisions were built between 1995 and 2015. The good news: wiring is generally modern Romex, panels are typically Square D or Eaton, and grounding is solid. The challenge: load capacity. A lot of Round Rock homes were built with 100A or 125A service to keep builder costs down, and they're now running into ceiling issues with EV chargers, hot tubs, dual HVAC, and induction ranges.

Roughly 60% of Round Rock projects we do are panel upgrades or EV charger installs. Many homeowners upgrade to 200A as part of an EV install — and we typically bundle the work to keep total cost lower than doing them separately.

Permitting in Round Rock goes through the Williamson County Building Department, with electrical permits issued through the City of Round Rock. We pull both. Typical permit-to-inspection turnaround is 5–10 business days.

For HOA neighborhoods (Forest Creek, Teravista, Walsh Ranch, Stone Canyon), we provide manufacturer specs and placement renderings as needed for HOA review of generators and exterior EV chargers.

FAQ — Round Rock

Round Rock-specific questions

What's the typical response time in Round Rock?
For non-emergency, same-day or next-day. For emergency dispatch from our central Austin office to Round Rock: ~40 minutes average, depending on traffic on I-35 and 130.
Are you licensed for Round Rock work?
Yes — Texas TECL #29874 covers all of Texas. We're registered with the City of Round Rock for electrical permits and have completed 320+ projects within Round Rock city limits since 2008.
Do Round Rock HOAs allow standby generators?
Most do, with reasonable placement and screening. We've installed in Forest Creek, Teravista, Walsh Ranch, and Stone Canyon — all approved. We provide manufacturer specs and a placement diagram for HOA review.
What permits does Round Rock require?
Electrical permits go through the City of Round Rock Building Inspections. Panel upgrades require a permit and final inspection. Generator installs require electrical + gas permits. We pull every required permit.
Do you serve Hutto and Pflugerville too?
Yes. Hutto is part of our regular service area, and we have a dedicated Pflugerville page as well.
