Cedar Park Specifics

Why Cedar Park homes need careful electrical work

Cedar Park is one of Texas' fastest-growing suburbs — population up 60%+ since 2010. Most homes were built between 1998 and 2018, in master-planned communities like Buttercup Creek, Twin Creeks, Anderson Mill West, and Cypress Mill. Wiring is generally modern Romex with Square D HOM panels, but those panels were sized for the build year, not today's loads.

EV ownership has exploded in Cedar Park — we've installed 70+ Tesla and ChargePoint chargers in the area in the last 18 months alone. Many of those installs revealed an undersized panel: a 125A panel running an electric range, two HVAC zones, hot tub, and a Tesla quickly hits its limit. We bundle panel upgrades with EV installs when needed.

Cedar Park electrical permits go through the City of Cedar Park Building Department. We pull permits and coordinate inspections directly. Typical permit-to-inspection turnaround is 5–7 business days for residential.

Generator demand in Cedar Park has been particularly strong post-Uri (2021). The combination of relatively rural pockets, longer power restoration times after storms, and tech-employee buyers willing to invest has made standby generators a top-three service for us in this market.

FAQ — Cedar Park

Cedar Park-specific questions

What's your typical response time in Cedar Park?
For non-emergency, same-day or next-day. For emergency dispatch from our central Austin office to Cedar Park: ~45 minutes average, depending on traffic on 183A and 1431.
Can you handle ADUs/casitas in Cedar Park?
Yes. Cedar Park ADU electrical typically requires a sub-panel or even a full second service. We coordinate with the City of Cedar Park Building Department on the residential expansion permit and any required PEC service drop coordination.
What permits does Cedar Park require?
Electrical permits go through the City of Cedar Park Building Department. Panel upgrades, generator installs, and Level 2 EV chargers all require permits. We pull every required permit.
Do Cedar Park HOAs allow exterior EV chargers and generators?
Most do, with reasonable placement. Twin Creeks, Cypress Mill, and Anderson Mill West HOAs have all approved generator and EV charger installs we've completed. We provide manufacturer specs and placement diagrams for HOA review.
Do you serve Leander too?
Yes. Leander is part of our regular service area — we serve Travisso, Crystal Falls, Caballo Ranch, and other major Leander communities. Response time is similar to Cedar Park.
