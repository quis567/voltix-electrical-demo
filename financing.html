FAQ

Financing questions

How fast is approval?
Most applications are approved within minutes via a soft credit pull (no impact to your credit score). For larger projects ($25,000+), a hard pull may be required at the final approval step — we'll tell you before that happens.
What credit score do I need?
Generally 640+ qualifies for our promotional 0% offers. 580+ typically qualifies for extended-term financing at standard rates. Lower scores may still be approved with a co-signer.
Are there fees?
No application fee. No prepayment penalty. Some programs charge a small documentation/origination fee (typically $0–$99) which is shown in the loan terms before you sign.
What happens if I don't pay off the 0% promo within 12 months?
If you don't pay the full balance during the promotional period, deferred interest is assessed from the original purchase date at the standard APR (typically 24.99–29.99%). This is true of most "0% promotional" offers — read the fine print. Most of our customers pay off within the promo term using the savings from the project itself (e.g., generator + outage avoidance, EV gas savings, panel + insurance reduction).
Can I combine projects?
Yes. If you're doing a panel upgrade plus EV charger plus generator, we'll bundle the entire project under a single financing program for one monthly payment.
Do you accept other payment methods?
Yes — cash, check, all major credit cards, ACH/wire. We don't pad prices for credit card use.
