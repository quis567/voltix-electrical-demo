About Voltix

18 Years. 2,400+ Projects. Zero Code Failures.

Voltix Electrical Services was founded in 2008 by Master Electrician James Reyes after a decade of journeyman work in central Texas. The mission was simple: do residential electrical the way it should be done — to code, on time, with no surprise charges. Today our team of 14 licensed electricians serves homes and businesses across Greater Austin.
