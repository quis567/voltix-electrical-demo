Our Story

From a single truck to a 14-person team — without ever compromising the work.

Voltix was founded in 2008 by Master Electrician James Reyes, who had spent the previous decade as a journeyman in commercial and residential electrical across central Texas. He started Voltix from a single truck in his East Austin garage with one stubborn rule: never cut corners on safety, even if it meant losing the bid.

Eighteen years later, that rule still runs the company. Voltix has grown to a 14-person team of licensed Master and Journeyman electricians, but every job is still performed to NEC code, every permit is pulled, and every install is inspected. We've never had a panel upgrade fail final inspection.

What's changed is the work itself. In 2008 we mostly did service calls, ceiling fans, and panel work. Today, EV chargers and whole-home generators are 35% of our business — Tesla certified our installers in 2021, and we became a Generac PowerPRO Premier Dealer in 2022. The future of residential electrical is electrification, and we're at the front of it for Austin.

What hasn't changed: a Master Electrician shows up to every install. We pull every permit. We give a flat-rate quote before we start. And we stand behind the work for two years.
