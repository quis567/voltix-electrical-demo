FAQ

Before you call

How quickly will you contact me?
Form responses: within 1 hour during business hours (Mon–Fri 8am–6pm). After-hours form responses are handled the next business morning. Phone calls are answered live 24/7.
Is the estimate really free?
For installs (panel upgrades, EV chargers, generators, rewires, lighting), yes — fully free. For diagnostic / troubleshooting calls, we charge a flat-rate diagnostic ($149 standard, $249 same-day, $349 emergency) which is credited toward repair costs.
What if I just have a quick question?
Call us. We genuinely don't mind walking through whether you need a panel upgrade for an EV install or whether your flickering lights are urgent. No-pressure conversation. We'd rather you have correct info than book the wrong service.
Do you serve my area?
If you're in Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Lakeway, Westlake, Bee Cave, Leander, Hutto, Manor, Buda, or Kyle — yes. For surrounding areas (Georgetown, Dripping Springs, Marble Falls), we travel for larger projects but typically not service calls. Call to confirm your address.
